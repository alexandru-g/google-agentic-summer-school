# Pixel Pirates Online

**Genre:** MMO naval combat | **Developer:** Kraken Interactive | **Released:** 2022 | **Price:** Free to play

Hoist the colors in a shared-world pirate MMO where the wind is real, the broadsides are earned, and every ship needs a crew that can actually talk to each other. Pixel Pirates Online is built around 4-player crewed vessels: helm, sails, cannons, repairs — coordinate or sink.

## Features

- Crewed ship combat for up to 4 players per vessel, from sloops to galleons
- Living ocean with wind simulation, storms, and the whirlpool event the Kraken's Maw
- Open-sea PvP, trade routes, and tavern shanties
- Seasonal content, including the Sea Serpent Season battle pass
- Progression: earn sails, cannons, and cosmetics through play or the in-game Gem Shop
- Free to play; optional Deluxe Charter subscription

## System Requirements

- **OS:** 64-bit, 2016 or newer
- **CPU:** Quad-core 2.8 GHz
- **RAM:** 8 GB
- **GPU:** TitanEdge 500 or better, 3 GB VRAM
- **Storage:** 20 GB
- **Network:** Broadband required; persistent online world
