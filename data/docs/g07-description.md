# Haunted Spreadsheet

**Genre:** Horror comedy sim | **Developer:** Cursed Cell Studio | **Released:** 2024 | **Price:** €9.99

It's 5:47 PM. Everyone else has gone home. The quarterly budget file you opened has 13 sheets, and you only made 12. Haunted Spreadsheet is a short-form horror comedy played entirely inside an eerily convincing office spreadsheet: audit the numbers, answer the cell comments, and try not to look at F13.

## Features

- A complete horror story told through formulas, macros, and passive-aggressive cell comments
- The pivot-table jump scare (we will not elaborate)
- The macro seance: contact the intern who never logged off
- Fiscal Year of Fear: a free post-launch chapter featuring the year-end audit
- New Game Plus with three additional endings and one secret ending hidden behind a formula
- Main story: roughly 2 hours; completionist runs considerably longer since the free update

## System Requirements

- **OS:** 64-bit, 2012 or newer
- **CPU:** Anything that can open a spreadsheet
- **RAM:** 4 GB
- **GPU:** Integrated graphics fine
- **Storage:** 2 GB
- **Headphones:** strongly recommended after sunset
