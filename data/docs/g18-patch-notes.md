# Farm & Forge — Patch Notes

## v2.0 — "Reforged" — 2026-03-12

- The big one. Mythril forging tier with 40 new craftable items
- Ranching system: highland sheep, storm goats, and pasture management
- Winter agriculture: frostwheat, icemelon, and heated greenhouse buildings
- New deep-mine biome below level 60
- A letter from Anvil & Acre: we went quiet for far too long. The silence after v1.9 was a small team rebuilding the engine underneath the game, but we should have said so. We are sorry. The roadmap page is back online, and so are we. Thank you for waiting — this update is for the veterans who never uninstalled.

*(Note: v2.0 arrived 14 months after the previous patch.)*

## v1.9 — "Hearthfire" — 2025-01-09

- Fireplace furniture set and winter cosmetics
- Minor bug fixes
- Roadmap teaser: ranching update "coming soon"

## v1.8 — "Deep Veins" — 2024-05-23

- Adamant metal tier and mine levels 40–60
- Autumn festival expanded with the pumpkin trebuchet contest
- Quality-of-life pass on tool switching
