# Rooftop Ramen

**Genre:** Restaurant management | **Price:** €11.99 | **Developer:** Broth Bros | **Released:** 2023

Run a tiny ramen bar on a city rooftop, one glowing floor at a time. Take tickets, balance broths, upgrade your stations, and climb from a folding table on floor 1 to the neon penthouse on floor 30 — all set to an original lo-fi soundtrack that never rushes you.

## Features

- 30 rooftop floors to unlock, each with new regulars and rush patterns
- Signature recipe system: master Midnight Miso, Neon Tonkotsu, Spicy Sunrise Shoyu and more
- Deep station upgrades: stoves, broth vats, noodle pullers, garnish rails
- Original 2-hour lo-fi soundtrack with dynamic day/night mixes
- Relaxed mode with no timers for pure vibes
- A rooftop cat who supervises every shift

## System Requirements

- **Minimum:** Windows 10, Intel i3-6100, 4 GB RAM, GTX 750 Ti / integrated graphics, 2 GB storage
- **Recommended:** Windows 10/11, Intel i5-9400, 8 GB RAM, GTX 1050 or better, SSD
