# Glacier Punk

**Genre:** Open-world survival | **Price:** €34.99 | **Developer:** Coldsnap Studio | **Released:** 2026

Survive on a living glacier that moves, cracks, and reshapes itself every in-game week. Glacier Punk is an open-world survival game about homesteading the most hostile terrain imaginable: scavenge the frozen city of Frosthaven, hunt caribou across shifting ice fields, and build a base that can survive the ground literally leaving from under it.

## Features

- A dynamic glacier world: crevasses open, ice shelves collapse, settlements migrate
- Full survival simulation: warmth, hunger, frostbite, layered clothing
- Base building with geothermal vents, ice anchoring, and modular interiors
- Story campaign including the quest lines "The Long Thaw" and "Whiteout Protocol"
- Aurora-lit skies and a fully dynamic weather system
- Climb the Shiverspire, the tallest ice spire on the map

## System Requirements

- **Minimum:** Windows 10, Ryzen 5 3600, 16 GB RAM, RTX 2060, 60 GB SSD
- **Recommended:** Windows 11, Ryzen 7 5800X, 32 GB RAM, RTX 4070, 60 GB NVMe SSD
