# Mecha Garden — Patch Notes

## v1.3.0 "Harvest Protocol" — 2026-03-30

- **New: dynamic weather system.** Rain, dry spells, and valley fog now affect growth rates, irrigation needs, and seasonal planning
- **New: crop mutations.** Cross-season strains for rustwheat and glowberries with visible trait inheritance
- Combat tuning, pass one: two new chrome aphid variants (burrower, swarm-caller), enemy aggression rebalanced. We know combat still needs deeper work — a fuller rework ships with Raid Blooms.
- **Roadmap update:** we owe you honesty. Co-op Greenhouses has moved to a 2026 Q4 target; Raid Blooms is in internal prototype. We announced both too early, and we're sorry. Neither is cancelled.

## v1.2.0 — 2025-12-10

- Combat first pass: new weapon mounts (tiller-lance, seed cannon), enemy telegraphs improved
- Storage: silo capacity doubled; sorting filters added
- Fixed drones watering the same plot twice while a neighboring plot wilted dramatically

## v1.1.0 — 2025-08-14

- Quality of life: batch planting, cockpit camera options, colorblind-friendly soil moisture overlay
- Full controller remapping support
- Fixed the Trowel-9 clipping through the greenhouse roof when jumping while extremely tall

## v1.0.2 — 2025-05-16

- Launch fixes: resolved a save-slot display issue and a rare crash at dawn transitions
- Glowberries now glow 20% more. You're welcome.
