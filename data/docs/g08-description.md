# Orbit Fall

**Genre:** Physics platformer | **Price:** €7.99 | **Released:** 2023 | **Developer:** Weightless Whale

Gravity is not your enemy. It's your only friend. Orbit Fall is a precision platformer built on a fully deterministic physics engine: momentum, arc, and orbital pull behave identically on every attempt, so mastery is always possible and every fall is a lesson. Fling yourself between gravity wells, thread the Slingshot Gauntlet, and survive the magnetic fury of Magnetron Prime on your way through the shattered moons of Kepler Junction.

## Features

- 120 hand-tuned levels across six gravity zones, including the Tidal Locks and Gravity Well 7
- Deterministic physics — same input, same result, every time. Beloved by speedrunners.
- Global leaderboards with ghost replays and a daily challenge run
- Adaptive soundtrack that intensifies the longer you fall
- Full controller support with per-axis deadzone calibration (added in v1.5.0)

## System Requirements

- **OS:** Windows 10 64-bit
- **CPU:** Dual-core 2.4 GHz
- **RAM:** 4 GB
- **GPU:** Any card with 1 GB VRAM; integrated graphics supported
- **Storage:** 900 MB
