# Neon Drift Racers

**Genre:** Arcade racing | **Developer:** Velocity Forge | **Released:** 2024 | **Price:** €19.99

Slide sideways through a rain-slicked neon metropolis in the arcade racer built around one thing: the perfect drift. Neon Drift Racers pairs a weight-transfer drift model — feather the throttle, flick the wheel, ride the slide — with an original synthwave soundtrack of 42 licensed-free tracks that syncs its beat drops to your corner exits.

## Features

- 14 circuits at launch, including Chromatic Bay, Sunset Loop, and the vertigo-inducing Skyline Spiral
- Championship mode with 8 cups, plus time trials with global leaderboards
- Drift ghosts: race your friends' best laps asynchronously
- 24 customizable cars across street, drift, and prototype classes
- Full soundtrack player in the garage

## System Requirements

- **OS:** 64-bit, 2018 or newer
- **CPU:** Quad-core 3.0 GHz
- **RAM:** 8 GB
- **GPU:** Vortex 2200 / TitanEdge 460 or better, 4 GB VRAM
- **Storage:** 25 GB
- Recommended for 144 fps: Vortex 3600 class GPU, 16 GB RAM
