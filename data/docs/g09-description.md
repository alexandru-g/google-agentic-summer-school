# Moth Light

**Genre:** Metroidvania | **Price:** €17.99 | **Released:** 2025 | **Developer:** Lantern Collective

Follow the last lantern-bearer into a kingdom that forgot the sun. Moth Light is a hand-drawn metroidvania about grief, light, and the things that gather around it. Every frame of animation is drawn by hand; every corridor of the Wax Cathedral, Cinder Hollow, and the Gloaming is painted like a storybook you shouldn't have opened. Beneath the beauty waits a mournful cello score and a story that has left even hardened genre veterans in tears.

## Features

- Fully hand-drawn art and animation — over 14,000 individual frames
- A haunting orchestral score built around solo cello, recorded live
- Duel-like boss encounters, including Mother Pyralis and the Chandler
- Deep movement kit with ability-gated exploration and sequence-break potential
- Bench-warp fast travel network and customizable map pins (expanded in update 1.1)
- 20+ hour story with a fully voiced ending sequence

## System Requirements

- **OS:** Windows 10 64-bit
- **CPU:** Quad-core 2.8 GHz
- **RAM:** 8 GB
- **GPU:** 2 GB VRAM (1050-class or better)
- **Storage:** 6 GB
