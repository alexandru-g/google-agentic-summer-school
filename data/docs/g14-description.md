# Mecha Garden

**Genre:** Mech farming sim | **Price:** €15.99 | **Released:** 2025 | **Developer:** Iron Petal

The war is over. The mechs stayed. In Verdigris Valley, decommissioned war machines have found a second life pulling ploughs, planting rustwheat, and watering glowberry fields by moonlight. Climb into your Trowel-9, tune its servos for soft soil, and build the farm you'd want to wake up inside. Mecha Garden is a cozy farming sim where every chore has a forty-ton solution — and where the rain on your cockpit hull is reason enough to stay out one more season.

## Features

- Pilot and customize the Trowel-9: tools, drones, paint, and shoulder-mounted flower boxes
- Crops with personality: rustwheat, glowberries, and mutation strains from the Harvest Protocol update
- Dynamic weather and seasonal planning (v1.3)
- Irrigation drone automation for late-game farms
- Light defense encounters against chrome aphids threatening your fields
- In development per public roadmap: Co-op Greenhouses (targeting 2026 Q4) and the Raid Blooms combat overhaul (prototype)

## System Requirements

- **OS:** Windows 10 64-bit
- **CPU:** Quad-core 3.0 GHz
- **RAM:** 8 GB
- **GPU:** 4 GB VRAM
- **Storage:** 10 GB
