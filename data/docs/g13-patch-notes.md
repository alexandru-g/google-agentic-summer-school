# Bureau of Time — Patch Notes

## v1.4.0 — 2026-03-20

- **New: Consultant Mode.** An optional, three-tier hint system — a nudge, a lead, and a full solution — available from the pause menu. Off by default; using it disables nothing and judges no one.
- Puzzle telemetry pass: the tram timetable poster is now referenced in the casebook after you first see it, and the pneumatic mail sorter shows sorting-rule feedback after two failed attempts
- Fixed Inspector Vell repeating a loop-three line during loop seven under specific dialogue orders

## v1.2.0 — 2025-02-11

- Telegraph cipher: an additional clue document now appears in the Records Annex after 30 minutes on the puzzle
- Deduction board: pins can now be color-coded; connections can be labeled
- Fixed a progression block when solving the stamp-and-pigeon chain out of intended order
- Performance improvements in the rain-heavy Annex exterior scenes

## v1.0.5 — 2024-11-28

- Added loop-skip after first completion for replay and study of the ending
- Quality of life: casebook search, faster dialogue skip on repeat loops
- Fixed 14 typos. The one on the poster in the tram office was intentional. It matters. That's all we'll say.
