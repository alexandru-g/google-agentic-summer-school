# Pixel Pirates Online — Patch Notes

## v4.1 "Event Horizons" — 2025-11-05
- Event weekends now run on dedicated high-capacity servers
- **Priority boarding:** Deluxe Charter subscribers skip the standard login queue during peak hours
- New cosmetic line in the Gem Shop: Ghostfire sail set

## v4.0 "The Admiral's Vault" — 2025-03-12
- New feature: Vault Crates — randomized armament rewards, including the Ghost Cannon and Serpent Chaser lines with unique stat profiles
- Gem bundle pricing restructured; conversion rates updated
- Vault-exclusive armaments are not obtainable through gameplay progression
- Balance: legacy cannons adjusted to make room for the new armament tiers

## v3.5 "Sea Serpent Season" — 2024-06-19
- Introducing the seasonal battle pass: 60 tiers of sails, figureheads, and emotes
- Daily streak quests grant bonus pass progress; streaks reset after 48 hours of inactivity
- Pass tiers expire at season end
- New event: the Sea Serpent surfaces in open water every 3 hours

## v3.0 — 2023-08-02
- Gem Shop expanded beyond cosmetics: XP boosts, repair kits, and fast-travel charters now available
- New trade routes in the southern archipelago

## v2.0 — 2023-01-20
- Server capacity doubled in response to launch-window login queues
- Queue position and estimated wait now displayed on the login screen
