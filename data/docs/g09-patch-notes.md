# Moth Light — Patch Notes

## v1.1.0 "Wayfinder" — 2026-03-12

Our biggest update yet, focused on the number one piece of community feedback: navigation.

- Added custom map pins — place up to 30 markers with six icon types
- Redesigned the map legend; room outlines now distinguish visited, cleared, and secret-remaining states
- The first bench-warp (fast travel) unlock moved from the Gloaming to early Cinder Hollow — roughly three hours earlier in a typical playthrough
- Bench-warp costs reduced by 50%; warping is now free after collecting the Compass Sigil
- Corridor signposts added at seven major junctions

## v1.0.3 — 2025-09-18

- Smoothed the difficulty spike in the mid-Gloaming gauntlet; enemy density reduced
- Fixed Mother Pyralis occasionally skipping her lullaby phase transition
- Fixed a soft-lock when quitting during the Chandler's second phase
- Audio: fixed cello theme cutting out when entering the Wax Cathedral from the east lift

## v1.0.1 — 2025-04-10

- Launch fixes: resolved a rare crash on ultrawide displays
- Fixed collectible counter displaying 99% at 100% completion
- Minor dialogue typo corrections
