# Wizard Landlord

**Genre:** Management sim | **Price:** €13.99 | **Developer:** Arcane Lease | **Released:** 2024

You inherited three crumbling magical towers and every cursed tenant inside them. Wizard Landlord is a management sim about keeping liches, imps, banshees, and one extremely litigious frog wizard happy, housed, and (theoretically) paying rent. Renovate haunted attics, mediate roommate disputes between werewolves, and read the finest complaint letters ever written in a video game.

## Features

- Dozens of tenant species, each with unique needs, grudges, and handwriting
- Procedurally seasoned complaint letters — the community's favorite feature
- Tower renovation: rebuild crypts, swamps, belfries, and penthouse lairs
- Rent, upkeep, and market simulation across three districts
- Eviction quest lines (the poltergeist will not go quietly)
- Tenant unions, guilt-ridden ghosts, and passive-aggressive vampires

## System Requirements

- **Minimum:** Windows 10, Intel i3-8100, 4 GB RAM, GTX 960, 3 GB storage
- **Recommended:** Windows 10/11, Intel i5-10400, 8 GB RAM, GTX 1650, SSD
