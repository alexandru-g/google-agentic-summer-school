# Castle Contractors — Patch Notes

- **UI overhaul:** doors, windows, and stairs now place in one click from a persistent hotbar
- Radial build wheel remembers your last selection
- Blueprint sharing: export and import castle designs
- Full input rebinding
- First pass on distant-structure imposters; a full level-of-detail system remains in development

- Reduced physics solver cost for static, settled structures
- Known issue: frame dips during large collapses are expected and under investigation
