# Silent Depths

**Genre:** Survival horror | **Price:** €21.99 | **Released:** 2025 | **Developer:** Abyssal Point

Eleven kilometers down, the research station Meridian went silent. You are the one they sent to find out why. Silent Depths is a survival horror game about pressure — the hull's, and yours. Navigate flooded decks by sonar, ration flares and suit fuel, and listen: the anglerkin hunt by sound, and the Tidewalker never stopped hunting at all. Binaural audio design means you will hear things breathing before you ever see them. Most players finish with the lights on. Nobody finishes with headphones off.

## Features

- Award-caliber binaural sound design; headphones strongly recommended
- Sonar-based navigation through the Meridian, the Brinefall, and the trench beyond
- Survival systems: flares, suit fuel, oxygen, and a save network of pressure lockers
- Story told through crew logs — quiet, devastating, fully voiced
- Manual save slots with backup rotation (added in v1.2)

## System Requirements

- **OS:** Windows 10/11 64-bit
- **CPU:** 6-core 3.2 GHz
- **RAM:** 16 GB
- **GPU (minimum):** 8 GB VRAM — with texture streaming on Medium (improved in v1.2)
- **GPU (recommended):** 12 GB VRAM for High textures at 1080p and above
- **Storage:** 45 GB SSD required
