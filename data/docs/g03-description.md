# Star Salvage Crew

**Genre:** Co-op space sim | **Developer:** Orbital Labs | **Released:** 2025 | **Price:** €24.99

Somebody has to clean up the derelicts drifting through Sector 7 — and it pays best when nobody asks where the cargo came from. Star Salvage Crew is a 1–4 player salvage sim about cutting into dead ships, managing oxygen lines, and dragging contraband home before the reactor you assumed was cold proves otherwise.

## Features

- **Friend Pass:** one purchased copy lets up to three friends join for free
- Physically simulated cutting, breaching, and cargo handling
- Ten explorable wrecks at launch, from gutted freighters to the flooded research vessel Anemone
- Ship upgrades: better haulers, longer O2 lines, quieter engines
- Proximity voice chat with vacuum muffling — atmosphere is the whole point
- Best played with 3 friends; solo mode available with drone assistant

## System Requirements

- **OS:** 64-bit, 2019 or newer
- **CPU:** Six-core 3.2 GHz
- **RAM:** 16 GB
- **GPU:** Vortex 4600 / TitanEdge 900, 6 GB VRAM
- **Storage:** 40 GB
- **Network:** Broadband required for co-op
