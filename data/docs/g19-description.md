# Speedrun Sandwich

**Genre:** Action cooking | **Price:** €8.99 | **Developer:** Deli Dash Games | **Released:** 2025

Build absurd sandwiches against absurd timers with people you're about to yell at. Speedrun Sandwich is a physics-driven action cooking game built for the couch: sprint between stations, toss ingredients across the kitchen, and pray the Tower of Bologna lands assembled. Best served with 2–4 players and no dignity.

## Features

- Couch co-op for 2–4 players, plus full online multiplayer
- Physics-based sandwich assembly: throw, stack, catapult, regret
- Legendary orders including the Gigaclub and the Tower of Bologna
- Unlockable kitchens with hazards (the fryer will catch fire, this is a promise)
- Solo score-attack mode for practicing your throws
- Accessibility options including camera shake toggle and motion reduction (added in v1.1)

## System Requirements

- **Minimum:** Windows 10, Intel i3-9100, 4 GB RAM, GTX 950, 2 GB storage
- **Recommended:** Windows 10/11, Ryzen 5 3600, 8 GB RAM, GTX 1060, SSD; controllers recommended for couch play
