# Farm & Forge

**Genre:** Farming / crafting | **Price:** €19.99 | **Developer:** Anvil & Acre | **Released:** 2022

Grow it, mine it, smelt it, smith it. Farm & Forge is the beloved farming-and-blacksmithing hybrid where the ore under your turnip field becomes the scythe that harvests it. Tend crops through four seasons, descend into an increasingly spooky mine, and master a forging minigame with real depth — your hammer timing matters.

## Features

- Full seasonal farming with weather-reactive soundtrack and festivals
- Blacksmithing: smelt ore into tools, weapons, and farm upgrades across six metal tiers, up to Mythril (added in the 2.0 Reforged update)
- Ranching system: raise, shear, and pamper highland livestock (new in 2.0)
- Winter agriculture with frostwheat and other cold-climate crops (new in 2.0)
- A mine that gets stranger the deeper you dig
- Hundreds of hours of content; a favorite of 500-hour veterans

## System Requirements

- **Minimum:** Windows 10, Intel i3-4130, 4 GB RAM, integrated graphics, 2 GB storage
- **Recommended:** Windows 10/11, Intel i5-8400, 8 GB RAM, GTX 1050, SSD
