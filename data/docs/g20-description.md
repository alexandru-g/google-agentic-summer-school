# Echo Tactics

**Genre:** Turn-based tactics | **Price:** €24.99 | **Developer:** Quiet Signal | **Released:** 2024

Your past turns never leave the battlefield. Echo Tactics is a turn-based tactics game built around the echo system: every move a unit makes leaves a ghostly imprint that both sides can exploit, so you fight the enemy and your own history at once. Every turn is a puzzle with three wrong answers and one beautiful one.

## Features

- The echo system: manipulate, erase, or replay past turns as a core mechanic
- 12 base-game units including the Null Warden and Chronomancer, each with deep skill trees
- Additional unit packs available as paid DLC, including the Iron Choir pack (€14.99)
- Handcrafted campaign with a precision difficulty curve
- Weekly puzzle missions and ranked multiplayer
- Built for thinky players: no timers in campaign mode

## System Requirements

- **Minimum:** Windows 10, Intel i5-8400, 8 GB RAM, GTX 1060, 25 GB storage (SSD strongly recommended)
- **Recommended:** Windows 11, Ryzen 7 5700X, 16 GB RAM, RTX 3060, 25 GB NVMe SSD
