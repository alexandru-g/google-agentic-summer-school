# Turbo Goat Simulator 9

**Genre:** Meme sandbox | **Price:** €6.99 | **Released:** 2024 | **Developer:** Chaos Pasture

The ninth entry in a series that has never once been asked for. You are a goat. The town of Bleatsburg is legally unprepared for you. Headbutt, lick, drag, and ragdoll your way through a physics engine we describe, with full sincerity, as "load-bearing duct tape." Clip through Mayor Crumb. Ascend via the trampoline church. Strap on a jetpack and ruin a wedding. The bugs are not mistakes — they are the content, and we patch around the funny ones very carefully.

## Features

- Fully destructible(ish) open town of Bleatsburg with five districts
- Industrial-strength tongue physics: lick it, drag it, regret it
- Jetpacks, trampolines, and the fan-favorite Goatnado event mode (v9.3)
- Challenge checklist and unlockable mutators for goal-oriented chaos (added in v9.1)
- Couch co-op party modes for up to four goats (v9.4)

## System Requirements

- **OS:** Windows 10 64-bit
- **CPU:** Dual-core 2.6 GHz
- **RAM:** 8 GB
- **GPU:** 2 GB VRAM
- **Storage:** 4 GB
- **Dignity:** none required
