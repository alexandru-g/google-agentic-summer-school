# Dungeon Sprouts — Patch Notes

## v2.1 "Pockets & Compost" — 2025-06-17
- **Inventory increased from 12 to 20 slots** — you asked, we sewed pockets
- New compost system: convert duplicate seeds into Rich Soil, a currency that speeds up late-game greenhouse unlocks (targeting the endgame grind feedback)
- Greenhouse tier costs reduced by roughly 30%
- Familiars can now carry one item each

## v2.0 "Deep Roots" — 2024-09-05
- New biome: the Overgrown Attic
- New boss: the Compost King, assembled from everything you discarded during the run
- 12 new seed types and 3 new trinket slots
- Rebalanced late-game encounter tables

## v1.5 "Familiar Faces" — 2023-08-15
- Familiar cosmetics: hats, scarves, and tiny boots for every companion
- Quality of life: auto-sort button for the seed pouch, faster menu transitions
- Grandma Bramble now apologizes when she defeats you (it was rude before)

## v1.0 — 2023-03-21
- Launch release: three biomes, 40 seed types, 6 bosses, one very small turnip
