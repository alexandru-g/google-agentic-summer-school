# Chess Royale 100

**Genre:** Strategy battle royale | **Price:** Free to play | **Released:** 2023 | **Developer:** Checkmate Cloud

One hundred players. One colossal board. One crown. Chess Royale 100 drops you onto a giant shared board where every player commands their own army in real time — and the board itself is shrinking. As the circle closes, entire files and ranks collapse into the void, forcing sprawling positional wars into desperate final-circle knife fights. Outlast, outthink, and claim the crown while 99 defeated ghosts spectate your endgame.

## Features

- 100-player simultaneous chess with a shrinking board and a shared final circle
- Ranked crown ladder with seasonal placement and spectator ghost mode
- Blitz Island limited-time mode: faster circles, no takebacks, pure chaos
- Fortress anti-cheat with server-side move analysis and regular ban waves (v2.0+)
- Replay review and community reporting tribunal (v2.5)
- Free forever: cosmetics only, no pay-to-win

## System Requirements

- **OS:** Windows 10 64-bit / any modern browser client
- **CPU:** Dual-core 2.0 GHz
- **RAM:** 4 GB
- **GPU:** Integrated graphics supported
- **Network:** Broadband required
- **Storage:** 1.5 GB
