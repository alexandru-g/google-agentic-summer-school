# Neon Drift Racers — Patch Notes

## v1.5 — 2025-09-30
- Added seasonal time trials with rotating track playlists and cosmetic rewards
- Drift ghost sharing: export a ghost link to friends outside your list
- Minor audio mixing fixes on Harbor Straight

## v1.4 — 2025-05-08
- **Stability:** Fixed a memory leak in the replay recorder (introduced in v1.3) that caused crashes to desktop and mid-race freezes, most frequently in the Midnight Interchange tunnel section
- **Stability:** Fixed a hang on the loading screen when the replay cache exceeded 2 GB
- Re-enabled automatic replay saving (disabled as a workaround in v1.3.1)
- Added crash telemetry opt-in to catch regressions faster

## v1.3.1 — 2025-03-02 (hotfix)
- Reduced the frequency of post-race crashes reported since v1.3
- Temporarily disabled automatic replay saving while we investigate the root cause

## v1.3 — 2025-02-11
- Two new tracks: Midnight Interchange and Harbor Straight
- New replay system with cinematic cameras and photo mode integration
- Drift ghosts now record full input data

## v1.2 — 2024-11-20
- Tuned AI catch-up assist on Expert difficulty (further tuning planned)
- Added photo mode
- Fixed collision jitter on Sunset Loop's banked hairpin
