# Castle Contractors

**Genre:** Building sim | **Developer:** Drawbridge Digital | **Released:** 2024 | **Price:** €29.99

You're not a king. You're the contractor kings call when they want a keep by Michaelmas and the moat dug by Tuesday. Castle Contractors is a physics-first medieval building sim where every wall bears real load — design it wrong and the collapse will show you exactly which stone betrayed you.

## Features

- Free-form construction: towers, gatehouses, banquet halls, regrettable balconies
- Structural physics with real load paths; collapses propagate believably
- Siege testing mode: bombard your own designs with catapults to find the weak points
- Contract campaign with 30 commissions, including the Baron's Folly chain
- Peasant workforce simulation with schedules, moods, and complaints
- Sandbox mode with unlimited stone

## System Requirements

- **OS:** 64-bit, 2019 or newer
- **CPU:** Eight-core 3.4 GHz (physics-heavy)
- **RAM:** 16 GB, 32 GB recommended for builds over 1,000 pieces
- **GPU:** Nova X1 / Vortex 4600, 6 GB VRAM
- **Storage:** 35 GB
- Note: very large castles are CPU- and GPU-intensive; see patch notes for ongoing optimization work
