# Dungeon Sprouts

**Genre:** Cozy roguelite | **Developer:** Tiny Cauldron | **Released:** 2023 | **Price:** €14.99

Grow a tiny vegetable hero, descend into the friendliest dungeon ever composted, and lose track of an entire evening — again. Dungeon Sprouts wraps deep roguelite build-crafting in a warm blanket: when a run ends, the game plants a memorial flower where you fell, and your garden grows a little wiser.

## Features

- 60+ seed types with a synergy system that rewards absurd combinations
- Charming bosses, from knitting-obsessed Grandma Bramble to the towering Compost King
- Four biomes, including the Mossy Cellar and the Overgrown Attic
- Adoptable familiars (yes, the turnip can wear a hat)
- Meta-progression garden that remembers every run
- No fail states that punish; losing is just gardening

## System Requirements

- **OS:** 64-bit, 2016 or newer
- **CPU:** Dual-core 2.4 GHz
- **RAM:** 4 GB
- **GPU:** Any card from the last decade with 2 GB VRAM
- **Storage:** 3 GB
- Runs comfortably on low-end laptops and handhelds
