# Bureau of Time

**Genre:** Detective adventure | **Price:** €18.99 | **Released:** 2024 | **Developer:** Clockwise Bureau

At 4:17 every afternoon, the city resets — and only you and Inspector Vell remember. Bureau of Time is a time-loop detective adventure where the same ten minutes hide an entire conspiracy. Interrogate suspects who don't know they've answered before, chase evidence through the Records Annex, and rearrange the case in your head loop after loop until it finally, terribly, makes sense. The ending has become something of a legend among players — the community's one rule is that nobody spoils it.

## Features

- Time-loop investigation: replay the 4:17 reset with everything you've learned
- Branching interrogations with a fully reactive deduction board
- Handcrafted puzzles, from the telegraph cipher to the pneumatic mail sorter
- Consultant Mode: optional tiered hints for stuck detectives (added in v1.4)
- 15–20 hour story with a twist ending players refuse to spoil
- Loop-skip and chapter select after first completion
